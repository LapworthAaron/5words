What Is 5WORDS?

5WORDS is a daily word‑puzzle challenge where you must uncover five hidden five‑letter words on a 5×5 board.

Each puzzle is uniquely scrambled, and your goal is to swap letters across rows and columns until every row forms a valid word.

It’s simple to learn, satisfying to solve, and perfect for fans of Wordle‑style logic games.

Check the help section to learn how to play.

Strategy Tips for Solving 5WORDS

Want to improve your score and reduce your number of checks?

  • Start by forming common word patterns (‑ING, ‑ATE, ‑ION, etc.).
  • Look for vowel placement — most English words follow predictable vowel spacing.
  • Make several swaps before checking to maximise efficiency.
  • Use the colour feedback to lock in greens early, then refine orange and pink tiles.
  • Try to solve each row independently, but remember that every swap affects the whole board.

Why 5WORDS Is Great for Your Brain

Word puzzles like 5WORDS help improve:

  • Vocabulary
  • Problem‑solving skills
  • Pattern recognition
  • Short‑term memory
  • Cognitive flexibility

It’s a fun, fast, and rewarding way to keep your mind sharp every day.

Challenge yourself, beat your personal best, and share your streak with friends.

Remember:

The squares will light up with 3 different colours to help guide you to the solution

  • Green: This means you have the correct letter in the correct spot, do not move this anymore

  • Orange: This means you have a letter that is in the correct row, but not the correct column

  • Pink: This means you have a letter in the correct column, but not the correct row
