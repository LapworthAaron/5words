About 5WORDS


5WORDS is a free daily word puzzle game created by Alban Software. Each day, a new puzzle is generated featuring five hidden five-letter words scrambled across a 5x5 grid.


Your challenge is to swap letters across the board until every row contains a valid English word. Colour-coded clues guide you towards the solution: green means a letter is in the right spot, orange means right row but wrong column, and pink means right column but wrong row.


Our Mission

We built 5WORDS to offer a fun, quick daily brain exercise that anyone can enjoy. Whether you are a word game enthusiast or just looking for a few minutes of mental stimulation, 5WORDS is designed to challenge and entertain.


How It Works

A fresh puzzle is available every day. Your progress, stats, and streaks are saved locally in your browser so you can track your improvement over time. Share your results with friends and see who can solve the puzzle in fewer checks.
